# Using Graphics Operator

Graphics Operator puts broadcast-style scenes on your stream and lets someone switch them live
from a second device while the scorer keeps scoring. It's a paid add-on — see
[Graphics Operator add-on](/billing/graphics-addon) for pricing and how to buy it.

There are three parts:

- **The graphics display** (`/display/graphics`) — one Browser Source in your streaming software
  that shows whichever scene is live.
- **Graphics Control** — where the graphics operator switches scenes.
- **Player Roster** — where you keep player headshots and bios.

Admins, Managers and Operators can use Graphics Control and the Player Roster.

## 1. Add the graphics display to your stream

The graphics display doesn't have its own row on the Outputs tab yet, so build its link from
another one:

1. On the match's **Outputs** tab, select **Copy URL** on the **Scorebug** display.
2. Paste it somewhere you can edit it and change `/display/scorebug` to `/display/graphics`. Leave
   everything after the `?` exactly as it is.
3. Add the edited link to OBS, vMix or Wirecast as a Browser Source at 1920 × 1080, placed over
   your camera.

Its background is transparent and it shows nothing until a scene is live. See
[Venue screens, OBS and vMix](/displaying-your-score/obs-and-venue-screens) for adding sources.

You set this source up once per match. Switching scenes never changes the link.

## 2. Open Graphics Control

On the match's control panel, open the **Outputs** tab and select **Graphics Control**. Open it
on a separate device from the one scoring — a second laptop or a tablet works well. It doesn't
take [control](/running-a-match/sharing-control) away from the scorer.

## 3. Switch scenes

Select a scene to put it on air. The live scene is highlighted. Select **Clear** to take
everything off.

| Scene | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Lower Third** | Both team names and scores in a bar, with up to three team statistics when a live data feed is connected. |
| **Player Stat Cards** | One player's match statistics. One button per player in the live feed. |
| **Player Headshot + Bio** | A player's photo and bio from your roster. One button per player in the live feed. |
| **Clear** | A blank output. |

Scenes use your match's [theme](/displaying-your-score/branding) — accent colour, font and
background tint.

## Where the player list comes from

<Callout>
  The player scenes need a live data feed. Player buttons appear only while a **ChampionData —
  JSON Feed** source is running in [Bridge](/connecting-the-bridge/championdata-and-updates) for
  the match. Without one, Graphics Control shows "No live player feed yet" and only **Lower
  Third** and **Clear** are available.
</Callout>

Statistics are mapped for netball, basketball and cricket.

## The Player Roster

The roster holds the photo and bio shown by the **Player Headshot + Bio** scene. It belongs to
your organisation and is reused across every match. Open it from the **Outputs** tab with
**Player Roster**. Roster upkeep is best done before match day.

### Adding a player

1. Select **+ Add Player**.
2. Enter a first and last name. Optionally add a **display name** (shown instead of the full
   name), and a **bio**.
3. Save, then select the round photo placeholder on the player's card to upload a headshot — PNG,
   JPG or WebP, up to 5 MB.

**Edit** and **Remove** are on each player's card. A player with a photo also has
**Remove photo**.

### Linking roster players to the live feed

A roster entry is matched to a player in the live feed by the feed's ID for that player, not by
name. While a feed is running, players it contains that aren't linked yet are listed under **Live
match — unmatched players**. For each one, either:

- choose an existing roster entry under **Link to existing player…** and select **Link**, or
- select **New player** to create a roster entry already linked to them.

Once linked, a player stays linked for every future match from the same provider. A player who
isn't linked still works in the Headshot + Bio scene — they show with their initials and no bio.

## Privacy: photos and bios are public

<Callout>
  Treat everything in the Player Roster as public. A player's name, photo and bio are shown on the
  graphics display, which anyone with the display link can open without signing in.
</Callout>

- **Photos** are stored at web addresses that don't need a login. Anyone who has a photo's address
  can open it.
- **Names and bios** can be looked up by someone who has your display link and the live feed's ID
  for that player. The roster can't be listed or browsed as a whole.

Only add photos and bios you have permission to publish. For junior players that usually means
consent from a parent or guardian, and your club, school or association may have its own rules
about publishing images of children — check them first. You're responsible for having that
permission.

To take something down:

- **Remove a player** — select **Remove** on their card in the Player Roster and confirm. They
  come off the roster, their name, photo and bio stop appearing in scenes, and their photo is
  deleted.
- **Remove a photo** — select **Remove photo** on the player's card and confirm. The photo is
  deleted and the player stays on the roster.
- **Remove a bio** — select **Edit** on the player's card, clear the bio and save.
- **Replace a photo** — select the photo on the player's card and upload a new one. The old photo
  is deleted.

A deleted photo is removed from ScoreHub's storage, so its old address stops working. A browser
or network that has already loaded the photo can keep showing its own saved copy for a while.

If you can't remove something yourself, or you need someone's details removed quickly, email
[hello@scorehub.co.nz](mailto:hello@scorehub.co.nz) and we'll delete it.

## If the add-on isn't active

Graphics Control and Player Roster show an **Unlock** screen, and the graphics display shows a
prompt to upgrade in place of scenes. An Admin can add Graphics Operator from the Account page.
