# Branding your displays

You can match your displays to your club or competition: team colours, team logos, an accent
colour, a background colour or image, a font, and a competition logo. Changes show on every display for the match
as soon as you make them.

| What | Where | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team colours | Control panel → **Settings** | All plans |
| Accent colour, background colour or image, panel transparency, font, text size | Control panel → **Theme** | Pro and Venue |
| Team logos | Control panel → **Logos** | Pro and Venue |
| Competition logo | Control panel → **Theme** | Pro and Venue |

## Team colours

On the **Settings** tab, the **Home Team** and **Visitor Team** cards each have a colour picker and
a row of presets, under the team's name. A team's colour is used for its score, colour strips and possession
indicator on every display.

## Team logos

On the **Logos** tab, each team has an upload area. Select it or drag an image onto it. **Replace
Logo** swaps the image and **Remove** clears it.

Logos appear on the larger scoreboard displays, such as Advanced and Fullscreen. Use a PNG or SVG
with a transparent background for the cleanest result.

## Theme

The **Theme** tab has a live preview at the top and these settings:

- **Primary / Accent Colour** — used for the clock, period and highlights.
- **Display Background Colour** — the background of the scoreboard displays. The overlay,
  scorebug and graphics displays always stay transparent.
- **Display Background Image** — an image that fills the Fullscreen, Basic and Advanced displays,
  over the background colour. It's cropped to fill the screen, so a 1920×1080 image fits best.
  The overlay, scorebug and graphics displays stay transparent so they still layer over video.
- **Score Panel Transparency** — how much of the background shows through the score panels, from
  0% (solid) up. Raise it when you use a background image you want to stay visible.
- **Display Font** — type the name of any [Google Font](https://fonts.google.com), or pick a
  suggestion. Condensed fonts such as Bebas Neue, Oswald or Barlow Condensed suit scoreboards.
  Leave it blank, or select **Clear font**, for the default.
- **Text Scale** — makes scores and clock text smaller or larger, from 0.5× to 2×. Useful when a
  screen is a long way from the crowd.
- **Competition / Event Logo** — shown in the header of displays that have one. Separate from team
  logos.

**Reset Theme to Defaults** puts the colours, font, text scale and panel transparency back and
removes the background image and competition logo.

## Image requirements

| | Formats | Maximum size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team and competition logos | PNG, JPG, SVG, WebP, GIF | 5 MB |
| Display background image | PNG, JPG, SVG, WebP, GIF | 5 MB |

## Things to know

- Branding belongs to the match. A new match starts with the default colours and no logos, so set
  them again, or apply them before the match starts.
- **Reset Match** keeps team colours, logos and the theme.
- On the Free plan, the **Logos** and **Theme** tabs show an upgrade message instead of their
  settings, and every display carries a small "Powered by ScoreHub" mark. Team colours still
  work. Upgrading to Pro or Venue unlocks both tabs and removes the mark from open displays
  within about a minute — no need to reload them. See [Plans & pricing](/billing).
- If a custom font doesn't appear, check the spelling matches the name on Google Fonts exactly.
