# Contact support

The quickest way to get help is to ask the help assistant. It answers from these help articles, and
if it can't settle your question it opens a support request that goes straight to the ScoreHub team,
with your conversation attached so you don't have to repeat yourself.

<AskButton>Ask a question or contact support</AskButton>

You'll be asked to log in to ScoreHub before sending a request, so it reaches us with your
organisation and plan attached. We reply by email.

**Can't log in?** Try to sign in at [app.scorehub.co.nz/login](https://app.scorehub.co.nz/login?support=1)
and use **Contact support** on that page, or email
[hello@scorehub.co.nz](mailto:hello@scorehub.co.nz).

To help us answer quickly, include in your request:

- Your organisation name.
- The sport and, if it's a live-match issue, the match ID or the `/display` or `/control` link.
- What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened.
- Whether you're using the Bridge app (and which source — Saturn/Vega or ChampionData) or scoring
  by hand.

## Before you get in touch

A few things are quick to check yourself first — see [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting)
for connection issues, display links not updating, and Bridge/console problems.

If everything has stopped at once, check [status.scorehub.co.nz](https://status.scorehub.co.nz/)
for a known incident — see [Is ScoreHub down?](/support/service-status).

## Billing questions

Admins can manage plans, invoices, and payment methods directly from **Account → Plan & Billing**
— see [Billing](/billing). For anything the self-serve portal doesn't cover (refunds, invoicing
requests, closing an account), email hello@scorehub.co.nz.
