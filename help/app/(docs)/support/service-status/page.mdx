# Is ScoreHub down?

Check [status.scorehub.co.nz](https://status.scorehub.co.nz/). It shows whether ScoreHub's
services are running and lists any incident we're working on.

If the status page shows everything working and you still have a problem, it's most likely the
network at your venue or one device. The next section helps you tell which.

## Is it ScoreHub, or my network?

Every display and control panel has a status badge in the top right: **LIVE**, **CONNECTING** or
**OFFLINE**.

| What you see | Most likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| One device shows **OFFLINE** or **CONNECTING**, others show **LIVE** | That device's connection. Check its Wi-Fi and reload the page. |
| Every device at the venue is affected, but a phone on mobile data shows **LIVE** | The venue's network. Move the scoring device to a phone hotspot. |
| Every device is affected, including a phone on mobile data | ScoreHub. Check the status page. |
| Badges show **LIVE** but the score isn't changing, with a **FEED STALE** badge | The scoring console or Bridge, not ScoreHub. See [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting). |

A quick test: open one of the match's display links on a phone with Wi-Fi turned off. If that
shows **LIVE**, ScoreHub is working.

## What's kept during an outage

**The match itself is safe.** The score, clock, period, team names, fouls, timeouts and everything
you set up are saved on ScoreHub's servers as you go, not in your browser. When a control panel or
display reconnects, it picks up where the match was.

A few things are only held while the service is running, and are reset if ScoreHub restarts:

- **Undo history.** **UNDO** can't step back through actions from before the restart.
- **Who has control.** If two people had the match open, the first panel to reconnect takes
  control. The other shows **VIEWING ONLY** and can select **Take Control**. See
  [Sharing control of a match](/running-a-match/sharing-control).
- **The live Graphics Operator scene.** The graphics display goes blank until the operator
  selects a scene again.

Sound cues, your display links and your tokens aren't affected.

## What to do during an outage

1. **Check the status page** so you know whether it's us.
2. **Leave your pages open.** Displays and control panels reconnect by themselves when the
   connection returns. A page that has been offline for a while shows **RELAY UNREACHABLE**; reload
   it once the status page shows ScoreHub is back.
3. **Keep a note of the score.** Write down score changes while the control panel can't connect.
   Don't rely on anything you select while the badge isn't **LIVE** having been saved.
4. **Check the score and clock when you're back.** Correct them on the control panel if the match
   moved on while you were disconnected.

If you use Bridge, it shows **RELAY UNREACHABLE — SWITCH TO MANUAL CONTROL** while it can't reach
ScoreHub, and reconnects by itself. You don't need to restart it.

## Telling us about a problem

If the status page shows everything working but every device is affected, email
[hello@scorehub.co.nz](mailto:hello@scorehub.co.nz) with the match's link and what the badges
show. See [Contact support](/support).
