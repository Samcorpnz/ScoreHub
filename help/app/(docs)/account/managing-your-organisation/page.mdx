# Managing your organisation

Your organisation is created when you sign up, with the name you entered and you as its first
Admin. This page covers changing it afterwards: renaming it, handing it to someone else, leaving
it, and closing it.

Several of these aren't self-serve yet. Where that's the case, email
[hello@scorehub.co.nz](mailto:hello@scorehub.co.nz) from the address on your account and we'll do
it for you.

## Finding your organisation's name

The organisation's name appears in the organisation switcher in the dashboard header, which only
shows if you belong to more than one organisation, and in invitation emails.

<Callout>
  The **Organization** card at the top of the Account page shows **your** name and your role in
  the organisation, not the organisation's name.
</Callout>

## Renaming your organisation

There's no rename option in the app. Email us with the current name and the new one. An Admin
needs to make the request.

## Adding a second organisation

You can't create another organisation from an account that already has one. An account gains
organisations by being invited to them — see [Inviting your team](/account/inviting-your-team).

If you run more than one club or venue and need a separate organisation for each, email us and
we'll set it up. Tell us whether the new organisation should share your plan or be billed
separately. See [Switching organisations](/account/switching-organisations) for moving between
them.

## Handing over to a new Admin

Every organisation must keep at least one Admin, and you can't change your own role. So a
handover is done in this order:

1. **Make the new person an Admin.** In **Account → Team**, change their role to **Admin**. If
   they aren't a member yet, invite them with the Admin role and wait for them to accept.
2. **Ask them to check billing.** The card on file stays as it is. The new Admin can replace it
   from **Account → Plan & Billing → Manage billing**. See
   [Invoices & payment methods](/billing/invoices-and-payment-methods).
3. **Have them change your role or remove you.** Once there's a second Admin, they can change your
   role to something lower, or remove you from the organisation.
4. **Replace tokens and links you held.** Bridge, Stream Deck and data feed tokens aren't tied to
   a person, so they keep working after you leave. The new Admin should revoke the ones you had
   and generate new ones. See [Roles & permissions](/account/roles-and-permissions).

## Leaving an organisation

You can't remove yourself. Ask an Admin to remove you from **Account → Team**. A Manager can
remove you if you're an Operator or Viewer.

If you're the only Admin, hand over first, as above.

Leaving one organisation doesn't affect your sign-in or any other organisation you belong to.

## Closing your organisation

There's no delete option in the app. To close an organisation and have its data removed:

1. Cancel any paid plan and add-ons first — see [Cancelling your plan](/billing/cancelling).
2. Email us from an Admin's address, naming the organisation.

## Who can do what

| Task | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Change a member's role, or remove a member | Admin. A Manager can for Operators and Viewers. |
| Promote someone to Admin | Admin |
| Ask us to rename or close the organisation | Admin |
