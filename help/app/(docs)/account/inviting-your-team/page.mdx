# Inviting your team

Admins and Managers can invite new members to their organisation from the **Team** panel on the
Account page.

## Sending an invite

1. Go to **Account → Team**.
2. Enter the person's email address and pick a role (Manager and Admin are only offered to Admins
   inviting — see [Roles & permissions](/account/roles-and-permissions)).
3. Send the invite. It appears in the **Pending invitations** list until it's accepted or expires.
   Select **Revoke** next to a pending invitation to cancel it — the emailed link stops working
   straight away.

The invited person receives an email with a link to `/invite/accept?token=...`. Following it:

- **If they already have a ScoreHub account**, they log in and the organisation is added to their
  memberships — the org switcher then lets them move between organisations.
- **If they're new**, they set a name and password on the same page to create their account and
  join the organisation in one step.

Invitations expire **7 days** after being sent. An expired or already-used invite link shows an
error; send a new one from the Team panel.

## Changing or removing a member

From the same Team panel, Admins and Managers can change a member's role or remove them, subject
to the same role limits described in [Roles & permissions](/account/roles-and-permissions) — a
Manager can only act on Operators and Viewers. You can't change your own role or remove yourself,
and an organisation must always keep at least one Admin.
