# Account overview

Your ScoreHub account is organised around **organisations** (orgs) — a club, association, or
venue — with **members** who hold a role in that org. Everything under `/account` in the app
manages this: your profile, your team, and [billing](/billing).

## Your organisation

Signing up creates your account and its first organisation together — there's no separate
"create an organisation" step. If you belong to more than one organisation (for example, you help
run scoring for two different clubs), an org switcher appears in the dashboard header so you can
jump between them. See [Switching organisations](/account/switching-organisations).

## Your team

Anyone with the **Admin** or **Manager** role can see a **Team** panel on the Account page listing
current members and pending invitations, and can invite new people or change existing members'
roles. See [Inviting your team](/account/inviting-your-team) and
[Roles & permissions](/account/roles-and-permissions) for what each role can do.

## Changing your name, email or password

The Account page has a card for each:

- **Display Name** — the name shown to your team and in the app header.
- **Password** — enter your current password and a new one (8 characters minimum).
- **Email Address** — select **Change email**, enter the new address and your current password,
  then follow the verification link we send to the new address. The link expires after 1 hour, and
  your login email doesn't change until you've followed it.
- **Passkeys** — sign in with a fingerprint, face, or device PIN instead of a password.

See [Signing in, passwords and passkeys](/account/signing-in) for the details, including what to do
if you've forgotten your password.

<Callout>
  Billing — plans, add-ons, and invoices — lives on the same Account page. See
  [Billing](/billing).
</Callout>
