# Signing in, passwords and passkeys

Sign in at [app.scorehub.co.nz/login](https://app.scorehub.co.nz/login) with your email and
password, or with a passkey if you've added one.

## Forgotten your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and follow the link. It's valid for **1 hour** and works once.
4. Choose a new password of at least 8 characters and select **Save new password**.

If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder and that you used the address you signed up
with. For security, the page shows the same "Check your email" message whether or not an account
exists for that address. If the link has expired, request a new one.

## Changing your password

When you're signed in, open **Account** and find the **Password** card. Enter your current
password and a new one of at least 8 characters, then select **Update Password**.

## Changing your display name

On the **Account** page, edit **Display Name** and select **Save**. This is the name your team
sees in the Team list.

## Changing your email address

On the **Account** page, under **Email Address**:

1. Select **Change email**.
2. Enter the new address and your current password, then select **Send verification link**.
3. Follow the link we send to the **new** address within 1 hour.

Your sign-in email stays the same until you follow the link. While a change is pending, the card
shows which address the link was sent to.

## Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of typing a
password. It's stored by your device or password manager and can't be guessed or phished.

### Adding a passkey

1. On the **Account** page, find **Passkeys** and select **Add a passkey**.
2. Give it a name so you can recognise it later, such as "MacBook Pro", and select **Continue**.
3. Follow your browser's prompt to create the passkey.

You can add more than one — for example, one for your laptop and one for your phone.

### Signing in with a passkey

On the sign-in page, select **Sign in with a passkey** and follow your browser's prompt. The
button only appears in browsers that support passkeys.

### Renaming or removing a passkey

Each passkey in the list has **Rename** and **Remove**. Remove a passkey if you no longer have the
device it was created on. Your password keeps working whether or not you have passkeys.

## New accounts and invitations

- **Signing up** — you choose your password after following the confirmation link we email you.
  See [Getting started](/getting-started).
- **Invited to an organisation** — the invitation link lets you sign in to an existing account or
  create a new one. See [Inviting your team](/account/inviting-your-team).

## Still locked out

Email [hello@scorehub.co.nz](mailto:hello@scorehub.co.nz) from the address on your account and
we'll help. See [Contact support](/support).
