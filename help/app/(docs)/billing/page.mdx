# Plans & pricing

Billing is managed from **Account → Plan & Billing**. Everyone in the organisation can see the
current plan and add-ons there, but only an **Admin** can change them or open invoices and payment
details. Prices are in NZD; the annual price on every plan is always 10 months' worth (2 months
free).

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | One live match at a time, no custom branding. |
| **Pro** | $89 | $890 | Concurrent live matches across your account, custom team logos, custom competition logo, custom sounds, fixture upload. |
| **Venue** | $349 | $3,490 | Everything in Pro, sized for high-volume, multi-court venues. |

Every new account starts on Free — no card required. Team colours, the display theme (accent
colour, background, font and text scale), every sport, every display type, Bridge, and Stream Deck
control are available on all plans.

## Add-ons

Add-ons bill separately on top of a Pro or Venue plan. They aren't available on Free.

| Add-on | Monthly | Annual | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Graphics Operator](/billing/graphics-addon) | $29 | $290 | Broadcast-style scene graphics: lower thirds, player stat cards, and player headshots with bios. |
| [Data Feed](/billing/data-feed-addon) | $39 | $390 | An authenticated feed of live match state for third-party graphics software. |

## A plan covers the whole account

Your plan and add-ons belong to your account, not to a single organisation. If your account has
more than one organisation, they share the plan — and the Free plan's one-live-match limit counts
live matches across all of them. See
[The Free plan's one-match limit](/billing/free-plan-limit).

## Choosing between Pro and Venue

Free's one-live-match-at-a-time limit is the main thing that pushes most organisations to Pro:
once you need two courts, two fields, or two streams running at once, Free won't allow the second
match to go live. Venue is the same as Pro but intended for venues consistently running many
concurrent courts.
