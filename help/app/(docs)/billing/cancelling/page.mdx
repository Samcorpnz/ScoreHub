# Cancelling your plan

From **Account → Plan & Billing**, an Admin cancels a paid plan with the **Downgrade to Free**
button. It doesn't take effect immediately — it schedules the subscription to end at the close of
your current billing period, and the panel shows a **Canceling** label and the date it cancels on.
You keep Pro/Venue features until then, and your account moves to Free automatically once it
lapses.

Once you're on Free, only one match can be live at a time, and logos, the display theme, sounds
and fixture uploads are no longer available. A theme or logos already set on a match stay as they
are but can't be changed, and displays show a small "Powered by ScoreHub" mark. Team colours keep
working.

## Changed your mind?

Up until the cancellation date, a **Resume subscription** button appears in place of **Downgrade
to Free** — using it keeps you on the same plan and interval without having to check out again.

## Cancelling an add-on

Each add-on has its own **Cancel add-on** / **Resume add-on** button, separate from your plan, and
works the same way — see [Graphics Operator add-on](/billing/graphics-addon) and
[Data Feed add-on](/billing/data-feed-addon).

## Closing your account entirely

There's no self-serve "delete organisation" option. Email
[hello@scorehub.co.nz](mailto:hello@scorehub.co.nz) if you want your organisation and its data
removed after cancelling.
