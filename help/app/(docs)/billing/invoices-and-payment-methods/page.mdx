# Invoices & payment methods

Payment details, invoices, and receipts are managed through Stripe's billing portal, not inside
ScoreHub itself. From **Account → Plan & Billing**, the **Manage billing** button takes an Admin
to your account's Stripe customer portal, where you can:

- Download past invoices and receipts.
- Update or replace your card on file.
- View your current subscription and renewal date.

Use your browser's back button, or the portal's return link, to come back to ScoreHub. Changes
made in the portal (like updating a card) take effect immediately and don't require anything
further on the ScoreHub side.

The plan panel itself also shows the card on file (brand and last four digits) and the email
address invoices are sent to.

<Callout>
  **Manage billing** only appears for Admins, and only once the account has checked out at least
  once. Managers, Operators, and Viewers can see which plan the account is on, but don't have
  access to invoices or payment methods.
</Callout>
