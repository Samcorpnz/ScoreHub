# Graphics Operator add-on

**Graphics Operator** adds broadcast-style scene graphics to your stream — a lower third, player
stat cards, and player headshots with bios — switched live from a second device, for productions
that want more than a plain scoreboard on stream.

| | Monthly | Annual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphics Operator | $29 | $290 |

It's an add-on, not a plan — it's added to your Pro or Venue subscription, so it renews with your
plan and appears on the same invoice. It's billed monthly or annually to match your plan.
It requires an active **Pro** or **Venue** plan; it isn't available on Free.

## Buying it

On the Account page, an Admin scrolls to the **Add-ons** card and selects **Add Graphics
Operator**. ScoreHub shows what it will cost — nothing today; the rest of the current billing
period is added to your next invoice — and you select **Confirm**. It's charged to the card already
on file, so there's no checkout, and the add-on works straight away.

## What's included

- Scene graphics: lower third, player stat cards, player headshots with bios.
- Per-output fonts, text sizes and backgrounds.
- Green and blue key versions of the transparent outputs, for hardware switchers.
- A data out link (JSON, XML or CSV) for vMix and OBS data sources.

## Using it

Once active, **Graphics Control** lets an operator switch what scene is showing, and
`/display/graphics` is the single stable Browser Source URL to drop into OBS/vMix/Wirecast — you
set that URL once and never need to change it again as scenes change. See
[Using Graphics Operator](/displaying-your-score/graphics-operator) for the scenes, the player
roster, and where player data comes from.

## Cancelling the add-on

Select **Cancel add-on** on the Account page. Your plan carries on as it is. Like
[cancelling a plan](/billing/cancelling), the add-on stays active until the end of the billing
period you've paid for, and isn't on your next invoice. Select **Resume add-on** before then to keep
it, with nothing extra to pay.
