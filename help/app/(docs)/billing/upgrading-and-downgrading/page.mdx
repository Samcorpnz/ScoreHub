# Upgrading & downgrading

From **Account → Plan & Billing** (Admins only), pick Monthly or Annual billing, then choose a
plan. Checkout happens in place on the Account page — you don't get redirected to a separate
Stripe-hosted page — and your new plan takes effect as soon as payment completes. Billing status
updates automatically within a few seconds; the page shows "Finishing up your upgrade…" while it
waits for confirmation.

## Upgrade offer and promo codes

When an account is eligible, a banner on the plan panel offers 20% off your first 3 months. It's
applied automatically at checkout and can be used once per account. When the offer isn't being
applied, checkout has a field for a promotion code instead.

## Moving from Pro to Venue

On Pro, the plan panel shows **Upgrade to Venue**. Pick Monthly or Annual and select it. The
Account page shows what the change will cost before anything happens, with any upgrade offer
you're eligible for already applied. Select **Confirm** to make the change, or **Cancel** to leave
your plan as it is. It's paid with the card already on file — there's no checkout — and your
existing subscription is changed, so you're never billed for two plans at once.

- **Staying on the same billing interval:** nothing is charged today. The prorated difference for
  the rest of the current period is added to your next invoice.
- **Switching from Monthly to Annual:** a new billing period starts, so the annual price is
  charged straight away, less a credit for the unused part of the current month. Your add-ons
  switch with the plan. If the card can't be charged, nothing changes.

An upgrade can't also move annual billing to monthly. Upgrade on annual billing, then switch to
monthly, which takes effect from your next renewal.

## One invoice for everything

Your plan and add-ons are on one subscription, so they renew on the same date and you get one
invoice for all of them, billed monthly or annually to match your plan.

## Moving from Venue to Pro, or from Annual to Monthly

On Venue, the plan panel shows **Switch to Pro**; on annual billing, **Switch to monthly billing**.
Both are downgrades, so they take effect at the end of the period you've already paid for. Nothing
is refunded and nothing is charged today: you keep your current plan until then, and the plan panel
shows the date it changes. From that date you're billed at the new plan's price, and your add-ons
move to the new billing interval with it.

Until then you can change your mind: select **Keep Venue** (or whichever plan you're on) on the
plan panel and your plan carries on as before. Downgrading to Free also cancels a scheduled change.

## Moving to Free

Select **Downgrade to Free** on the plan panel. This cancels your paid plan at the end of the
current billing period, not immediately, so you keep paid features until then — see
[Cancelling your plan](/billing/cancelling).

## If a payment fails

The plan panel shows a **Payment issue** label when a renewal payment hasn't gone through. Your
plan keeps working for 14 days while the payment is retried. Select **Manage billing** to update
your card — see [If a payment fails](/billing/failed-payments) for the full timeline.
