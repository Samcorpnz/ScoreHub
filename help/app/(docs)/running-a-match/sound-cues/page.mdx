# Sound cues

A sound cue plays an audio file automatically when the match clock reaches a time you choose — a
two-minute warning, a hooter at full time, a shot-clock buzzer. Sound cues are set up on the
control panel's **Audio** tab and need a **Pro** or **Venue** plan.

## Adding a cue

1. Open the **Audio** tab.
2. Give the cue a **label**, such as "2-minute warning". If you leave it blank, the file name is
   used.
3. Choose which **period** it applies to — **Period 1** to **Period 4**, or **All periods**. For
   sports with more than four periods, such as a five-set volleyball match, use **All periods**.
4. Enter the **clock time** that triggers it, as `MM:SS`. For a hooter at the end of a countdown
   period, use `00:00`.
5. Choose an **audio file** from your computer, up to 8 MB.
6. Select **Add Cue**.

The cue appears in the **Sound Cues** list. Select **▶** to test it, or **✕** to remove it.

## When a cue plays

A cue plays once each time the running clock passes its time — counting down through it for
countdown sports, or up through it for count-up sports. It doesn't play when you set or adjust the
clock while it's stopped.

## Where the sound comes from

<Callout>
  The sound plays from **the computer that has the control panel open**, through that computer's
  speakers or audio output. Displays and overlays don't play cues.
</Callout>

To get cues into the venue PA or your stream, connect the scoring computer's audio output to the
PA, or capture that computer's audio in your streaming software.

Browsers block sound until you've interacted with the page. Select **▶** on a cue once after
opening the control panel to make sure audio is allowed.

## Cues are saved on that computer

Your cue list is stored in the browser you set it up in. A different computer, a different
browser, or a browser whose site data has been cleared starts with an empty list, and you'll need
to add the cues again. Set cues up on the computer you'll score from on match day.

Cues aren't tied to one match — they apply to every match you score from that browser.

## Troubleshooting

- **"requires the Pro or Venue plan"** — sound uploads aren't available on Free. See
  [Plans & pricing](/billing).
- **Nothing plays** — check the Audio tab was set up in this browser, the computer isn't muted,
  and that the cue's period matches the current period number.
- **File won't upload** — it must be an audio file (MP3 or WAV are safest) of 8 MB or less.
