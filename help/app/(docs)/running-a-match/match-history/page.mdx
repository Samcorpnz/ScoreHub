# Match history and final scores

When you select **End Match**, the match moves to the dashboard's **History** tab. This page
covers what History keeps, how to see a finished match's score, and how to get results out.

## What History shows

Each ended match is listed with:

- the two team names
- the sport and competition
- the scheduled time, if it had one
- the time it was ended

The sport and competition filters and the team-name search work on History the same way they do
on the other tabs. Matches are listed by scheduled time, earliest first; matches that were never
scheduled come after those, newest first.

<Callout>
  History doesn't show the final score yet. See the next section for how to find it.
</Callout>

## Seeing a finished match's final score

The final score, clock and period are saved with the match. There are two ways to see them.

**Open one of the match's display links.** A display link keeps working after the match has
ended and shows the final score. History doesn't have a **Copy display link** button, so this
works if you still have a link for that match — in your streaming software, on a venue screen, or
saved somewhere. If you'll want to refer back to a match, copy its display link before you end it.

**Reopen the match.** Select **Reopen** on the match in History. It becomes live again and its
control panel opens with the score as it was left. Select **End Match** on the **Settings** tab
when you've finished, or the match stays live.

On the Free plan a reopened match counts as your one live match, so you can't reopen one while
another match is live. See [The Free plan's one-match limit](/billing/free-plan-limit).

## Correcting a result

Reopen the match, make the change, and end it again. See
[Correcting a match after it has ended](/running-a-match#correcting-a-match-after-it-has-ended).

## How long matches are kept

Ended matches stay in History. ScoreHub doesn't remove them after a set time, and there's no
button to delete one.

If you need a match or its data removed, email
[hello@scorehub.co.nz](mailto:hello@scorehub.co.nz).

## Exporting results

There's no export yet — no CSV or PDF download, and no public results page. For now, record
results from the display or the control panel at full time.

If you need match data in another system while a match is live, the
[Data Feed add-on](/billing/data-feed-addon) gives other software a feed of the live match state.

## Who can see History

Everyone in the organisation can see the History tab. Admins, Managers and Operators can reopen a
match; Viewers can't.
