# Sharing control of a match

Only one control panel can score a match at a time. This stops two operators — or two browser tabs
— from changing the same score at once. Anyone else with the match open can watch it update live,
and can take over when they need to.

## Who's in control

The control panel's header shows where you stand:

- **IN CONTROL** — your panel is the one scoring the match.
- **VIEWING ONLY** — another panel is in control. You see the score and clock update, but your
  buttons don't change anything.

The first control panel to open a match gets control.

## Taking over

If you open a match that someone else is already scoring, an orange banner says **Another control
panel is already connected to this match.** Select **Take Control** to take over. Your panel
switches to **IN CONTROL** straight away.

The other operator's panel switches to **VIEWING ONLY** and shows a red banner: **Control was
taken by another operator. You are now viewing only.** They can select **Reclaim Control** to take
it back.

<Callout>
  Taking control doesn't ask the other operator first. Agree who's scoring before the match so two
  people don't keep taking it from each other.
</Callout>

## Handing over mid-match

1. The new operator opens the match from the dashboard with **Open Control**.
2. They select **Take Control**.
3. The previous operator can leave their panel open to watch, or close it.

Nothing is lost in the handover — the score, clock and period live on ScoreHub's servers, not in
either browser.

## What doesn't compete for control

- **Displays** never take control. Open as many as you like.
- **Graphics Control** and the **Player Roster** (part of the
  [Graphics Operator add-on](/displaying-your-score/graphics-operator)) run alongside the scoring
  operator without taking control from them.
- **Bridge** and **Stream Deck** send updates independently of who holds control on screen.

## Common situations

**I have two tabs open and one says VIEWING ONLY.** That's expected — your own second tab counts
as a second panel. Close the one you don't need, or select **Take Control** in the one you want to
use.

**My buttons stopped working mid-match.** Check the header. If it says **VIEWING ONLY**, someone
else took control; select **Reclaim Control**. If the connection badge says **OFFLINE**, see
[Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting).
