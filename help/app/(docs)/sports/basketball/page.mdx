# Basketball

Basketball matches run as 4 quarters of 10:00 each on ScoreHub's basketball template.

## Match structure

- Periods: 4 × QTR
- Clock: 10:00 countdown per quarter
- Timeouts: 5 per team

## Scoring buttons

- **FT (1)** — a free throw
- **2PT (2)** — a field goal from inside the three-point line
- **3PT (3)** — a field goal from beyond the three-point line

## Fouls and overtime

Each team has a **Fouls** counter under its score buttons. Selecting **End QTR** resets both
teams' fouls to zero for the next quarter. Any period after the fourth is treated as overtime and
starts with 5:00 on the clock instead of 10:00.

## Running the match

Score the match from the buttons above on `/control`. See [Getting started](/getting-started) for
creating the match and finding the control panel link.
