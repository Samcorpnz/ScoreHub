# Sports

ScoreHub ships a scoring template for each sport below — match structure, clock behaviour, and
score buttons are pre-configured, so setup is just picking the sport in the [match wizard](/getting-started).
Cricket and softball additionally get a dedicated ball-by-ball or innings control panel instead of
plain score buttons, and indoor cricket adds a wicket button to the standard panel.

Pick a sport for its specific structure, scoring buttons, and any match-format options:
