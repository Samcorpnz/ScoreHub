# Netball

Netball matches run as 4 quarters of 15:00 each on ScoreHub's netball template, or as 4 quarters
of 6:00 when you choose the Fast5 format.

## Match structure

- Periods: 4 × QTR
- Clock: 15:00 countdown per quarter (6:00 for Fast5)
- Timeouts: 1 per team

## Scoring buttons

- **1** — a standard goal from within the goal circle
- **2** — a Super Shot, worth double, for competitions that play a two-point shot

Fast5 has its own buttons — see [Fast5](#fast5) below.

## Match setup options

When creating the match ([Getting started](/getting-started)), choose the **Format**:

- **Standard** — 7-a-side, 4 quarters of 15:00
- **Fast5** — 5-a-side, 4 quarters of 6:00, with 1, 2 and 3-point shots and a Power Play

You can change the format afterwards on the **Settings** tab, under **Format**. That's also where
to set it for a match that was uploaded as a fixture, which starts as Standard. If the match
hasn't started, the clock moves to the new quarter length with it.

## Fast5

The Fast5 format follows the World Netball Rules of Fast5.

Each team has three score buttons, named for where the shot was taken from:

- **INNER (1)** — a shot from the inner circle
- **OUTER (2)** — a shot from the outer circle
- **SUPER (3)** — a super shot, from outside the goal circle

### Power Play

The last 2:00 of every quarter is the Power Play, when outer and super shots are worth double.
ScoreHub follows the clock: as it reaches 2:00 the buttons change to **1**, **4** and **6**, the
control panel shows **⚡ POWER PLAY**, and displays flag it next to the clock. At the start of the
next quarter the buttons go back to 1, 2 and 3. There is no Power Play in extra time.

The value depends on when the shot left the shooter's hands. If a shot taken just before the Power
Play goes in just after it starts, the button will already have doubled — score it, then take the
difference off with the minus buttons.

<Callout>
  Scorers also sound a signal when the Power Play starts. Add a [sound cue](/running-a-match/sound-cues)
  for **All periods** at `02:00` to play it automatically.
</Callout>

## Extra time

When you select **END QTR** at the end of the fourth quarter, displays show **FULL TIME** and the
clock is set for extra time. If the match is over, end it from the Settings tab as usual. If it's
going to extra time, start the clock — each period after the fourth shows as **ET 1**, **ET 2**
and so on.

Each extra-time period starts on:

- **7:00** for Standard netball — two halves, then play on until one team leads by two goals
- **1:00** for Fast5 — one period, then play on until the next goal

Competitions often set their own length. To change it, type the new length under **Clock & Match
Controls → Extra time** on the Score tab and select **Set**. See
[Extra time](/running-a-match#extra-time) for more.

## Running the match

Score the match from the buttons above on `/control`. See [Getting started](/getting-started) for
creating the match and finding the control panel link.

## On the display

When the match is fed by a ChampionData source through
[Bridge](/connecting-the-bridge/championdata-and-updates), viewers on `/display/advanced` see an
extra netball stats panel alongside the base scoreboard. Matches scored by hand or from a console
show the standard scoreboard only. See [Displaying your score](/displaying-your-score) for the
full list of display routes.
