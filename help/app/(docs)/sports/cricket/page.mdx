# Cricket

Cricket matches run as T20, ODI, or Test on ScoreHub's cricket template.

## Match structure

- Periods: 1 × INNINGS (the display label advances through 1st/2nd/3rd/4th innings as the match
  progresses)
- Clock: counts up from 0:00, no fixed length

## Scoring buttons

- **1, 2, 3, 4, 6** — runs scored off a delivery

## Match setup options

When creating the match ([Getting started](/getting-started)), choose the **Format**:

- **T20** — 20 overs per side
- **ODI** — 50 overs per side
- **Test** — up to 2 innings per side, multi-day

<Callout>
  For cricket you also enter starting batting/bowling squads in the match wizard — see
  [Getting started](/getting-started).
</Callout>

## Running the match

Cricket has its own Score tab on the control panel. You record each ball, and ScoreHub keeps the
score, overs, batter and bowler figures, strike and extras for you.

### Before the first ball

Enter both squads in the setup wizard — up to 11 names each. A team needs at least two players
to bat and the fielding side at least one to bowl. The home team bats first.

On the Score tab, select **Start innings**. The first two players in the batting squad open, and
the first player in the fielding squad bowls. The batter on strike is marked with `*`.

### Recording a ball

1. If the ball is an extra, choose its type first: **Wide**, **No-ball**, **Bye** or **Leg-bye**.
   Leave it on **Normal** otherwise.
2. Select the runs: **•** for a dot ball, or **1**, **2**, **3**, **4** or **6**.

The extra type returns to **Normal** after each ball. **This over** shows the balls recorded so
far in the current over.

ScoreHub handles the rest:

- Wides and no-balls don't count towards the six balls of the over.
- Strike changes on odd runs, and at the end of each over.
- After six legal balls the over completes, the bowler's figures update, and a maiden is counted
  if no runs came from it.
- After a no-ball, **Free Hit** shows for the next ball. On a free hit only a run out counts as a
  dismissal.

### Wickets

1. Select **Wicket**.
2. Choose how the batter was out: bowled, caught, lbw, run out, stumped, hit wicket, obstructed
   field or handled ball.
3. Choose the **next batter** from the list.
4. Select **Confirm Wicket**.

### Changing the bowler

Under **Bowling**, choose a player from **Change bowler…** and select **Set**. Do this at the end
of each over — ScoreHub doesn't choose the next bowler for you.

### Changing innings

An innings is complete after 10 wickets, or when the overs run out (20 for T20, 50 for ODI). The
panel then shows **Innings complete** and a **Start next innings** button. In the second innings
of a T20 or ODI, the panel shows the target and the runs and balls remaining.

### Test matches

Test matches add:

- **Declare** — ends the batting side's innings.
- **Follow-on** — when the side batting second finishes 200 or more runs behind, the panel offers
  **Enforce follow-on** or **Waive**.
- **Day/session** — set the day (1 to 5) and session (morning, afternoon, evening) shown with the
  score.

A Test innings has no overs limit; it ends on 10 wickets or a declaration.

### Fixing a mistake

**UNDO** under **Match Controls** reverses the last ball or action. The same section lets you
change team names and the match name. See the note on **Reset Match** in
[Using the control panel](/running-a-match) before using it — for cricket it clears the squads
and format.

## On the display

Viewers on `/display/advanced` see an extra cricket stats panel alongside the base scoreboard —
see [Displaying your score](/displaying-your-score) for the full list of display routes.
